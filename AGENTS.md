# Major Winners

A Counter-Strike legends draft game. Roll five historical Major team cards plus a coach, fill the role slots, then simulate a Major run and try to go 9-0.

## Stack

Astro (static) + React islands + Tailwind CSS v4 + TypeScript strict. The UI typeface is Stratum 2 when installed locally, with self-hosted Chakra Petch as the webfont. Tests are Vitest. Lint/format is Biome. Deploy is Cloudflare Workers static assets (`wrangler.jsonc`, `output: "static"`, no adapter) at `https://major.lorenzognech.workers.dev`.

No live HLTV (or other) network dependency for player data. Datasets are committed JSON under `src/data/`. Community features (save team, global boards, Versus community, Duel) talk to Supabase from the browser with `PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` and `PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`. Auth is optional magic link; publishing works anonymously. Duel rooms use per-player secrets and security-definer RPCs (the anon key cannot write `duels` rows directly). If those env vars are missing, community UI is hidden. Run stats on the boards are client-submitted; team OVR is recomputed from roster IDs against the committed dataset. Duel series are client-simulated. Casual private-match W-L inserts into `duel_results` (unique per user and room). Ranked 1v1 requires sign-in; Elo lives on `profiles` and is applied only by `submit_ranked_result` when both sides report complementary scores, which also updates wins, losses, and current win streak. The Elo board is public `profiles` ordered by rating. Two colluding accounts can still farm ranked because the sim is local; both-agree stops a single client from self-awarding a win.

## Engine invariants

- Engine modules import neither React, React DOM, Astro, DOM APIs, nor Supabase. UI is a thin renderer of engine output. Fetch and auth live in `src/community/` and components.
- All randomness flows through `src/engine/rng` (Mulberry32). `createRng` takes a uint32 or string (FNV-1a). `seedFromUtcDate` hashes the UTC `YYYY-MM-DD` day. A given seed must produce the same draft, coach order, and opponents. Match results match for the same seed, map queue, and per-map game plans, including coach-called timeouts.
- Prefer reducers and pure functions. Side effects (localStorage, fetch) live outside `src/engine/`.
- Invalid draft actions return `{ ok: false, error: DraftError }` and leave state unchanged. `startDraft` throws `DraftError` when the pool cannot supply five Major cards or a coach. `movePlayer` reseats a player already on the live roster; it does not advance the round.

## Draft

Six rounds. Each of the first five rolls a distinct completed Valve Major, then samples one of its participating five-player rosters with integer tier weights: `legendary` 5, `strong` 3, `cult` 1 (`TIER_WEIGHTS`). A 5% roll may replace one Major with a pre-2013 Legacy wildcard. The sixth round picks one coach from five sampled from the full pool (`COACH_CANDIDATE_COUNT`). Optional `tier: "legendary"` coaches are always included in that slate (sorted before the sampled remainder) and shown with gold legendary chrome.

- A draft has two Major rerolls and two team rerolls. The Major reroll keeps the current org and samples a different Major appearance of that org; the team reroll keeps the current event. A Major reroll is illegal (and the UI disables it) when that org has no other unused Major appearance. Both are legal only before the current player pick, exclude already shown cards, and derive from the root seed, round, action type, and which of the two uses.
- `RolledOrgYearCard` snapshots its Major/Legacy identity and five players. `applyAction` needs the committed dataset only for reroll actions.
- Completed state is five picks covering every role plus a coach.
- Player-round UI reveals each committed card with a majors reel, then a teams reel (`DraftRoll`). Both reels show imported Liquipedia logos (Major infobox image, org crest). The Legacy wildcard uses the committed CS 1.6 mark (`public/logos/majors/cs16.png`) instead of a Liquipedia event crest. The engine already chose the card; the animation is presentation-only. The live roster is hidden during the reel. After both reels lock, the result holds, then the pick board shows large team and Major crests beside the player cards and live roster. The team reroll sits under the org crest; the Major reroll sits under the event crest. Player cards are stacked rows: a year portrait (or initials) rail, nick with any revealed trait as a larger glowing icon beside the name (hover or click shows the trait title and `Each round has a small chance to` plus the catalog verb phrase), flag, primary/secondary role pills, year-LAN stats, and OVR. Eligible season traits (`src/data/json/player-bonuses.json` + `src/engine/bonuses.ts`) each appear on that rolled card with independent 50% probability from a derived seed (`${seed}:trait-reveal:${round}:${rerollIndex}:${seasonId}:${traitId}`), so Daily player/coach order is unchanged. Hidden traits cannot proc. Every bonus uses the shared plus mark (`public/bonuses/bonus.svg`) and glows green; every malus uses the shared minus mark (`public/bonuses/malus.svg`) and glows red. Statted cards show Rating, KPR, and DPR separately, plus ADR, KAST, and Impact when the season has them. Seasons without HLTV stats show Aim and Clutch instead. Each stat and OVR is colored independently on a red→green scale against the p10–p90 of committed Major-participant LAN seasons (Rating 1.0 and 2.0 use separate bands; DPR is inverted). That pool is the presentation reference, not teammates and not the elite OVR formula. Placement is `pickPlayer` / `movePlayer` with no confirm step: drag onto a role, tap a player then tap a role (or the role chips on the selected card), or focus a card/roster player and press 1–5 for AWP through lurker. An empty slot seats them; an occupied slot swaps; fit is recalculated. Below `lg` (1024px) the full sidebar roster is replaced by a sticky compact roster bar so cards stay on screen and vertical scroll still works (`touch-action: pan-y` until a drag arms). At `lg+` the live roster stays a sticky sidebar and pointer drag is unchanged. Placed players can be moved at any time during the draft, including the coach and locked-in screens. Restored Daily attempts skip the current card, `prefers-reduced-motion` skips it, a team reroll spins only the team reel, and a Major reroll spins only the majors reel. A Major reroll keeps the org crest on the team reel and withholds the new year and placement until that Major locks, so the event is not spoiled. Skip/continue is offered on the reveal.

## Roles

Starters: `awp`, `igl`, `entry`, `support`, `lurker`. Off-role placement is always allowed (fit `1.0` / `0.9` / `0.75`). A sixth draft round is the coach.

`primaryRole` is inferred per roster, not copied from Liquipedia TeamCard `p1`–`p5` listing order. That order is only a weak prior. HLTV does not publish a stable role field.

- `roleProvenance.kind` is `teamcard-slot` (no signal), `inferred` (career tags, optional Liquipedia infobox Role, opening/impact/ADR, uniqueness), or `curated`.
- Typical fives get one IGL and one primary AWP. Hand-locked rows live in `src/data/json/role-overrides.json` and always win.
- `npm run roles:infer` fills `openingKpr` from the HLTV cache when present, assigns inferred roles, then applies overrides. `npm run roles:apply` reapplies overrides only. Majors import and `hltv --apply` must not change curated roles or imported photos.

## Player strength

Each `PlayerSeason` carries `dataRegime`: `full` (CS:GO 2016+ / CS2 stats), `partial` (CS:GO 2013–2015 Rating 1.0), `none` (1.6 / Source accolade rubric), or `fallback` (Major participant without verified individual stats). OVR is relative to contemporaries, not raw HLTV rating.

Contemporaries means a **documented elite LAN-pool distribution per rating version** (`src/engine/ratings/reference.ts`), not the other four people on the same org-year card. Z-scoring inside a roster would punish IGLs.

- `full` / `partial`: z-score season rating against that reference, map to OVR, then add a **modest accolade bonus capped at +5**.
- `none`: use `curated.ovr` as-is. Do not add the accolade bonus again; the rubric already priced trophies.
- `fallback`: use a conservative placement-based curated OVR. `ratingProvenance.kind` makes estimates explicit; fallback OVR is capped below verified all-time peaks. Role may still be inferred or TeamCard-provisional independently of the rating path.
- Attributes (aim, entry, clutch, utility, consistency, igl) are what the sim consumes. Derived from stats when present; `curated.attributes` overlays. `igl` is role-driven unless curated.

`npm run calibrate` prints the top leaderboard and fails if s1mple-2018-navi, olofmeister-2015-fnatic, and heaton-2003-sk leave the top 15, if the top 10 is only CS:GO/CS2, or if fallback ratings crowd out verified legends.

## Team profile

`buildTeamProfile` is the pure boundary from a `CompletedDraft`, player-season metadata, rated players, and the selected coach into the profile consumed by simulation. It exposes the five component scores, aggregate fit-adjusted attributes, tactical coach scores, strengths/weaknesses, and overall team OVR.

- Base strength is the five-player average of `player OVR × assigned-role fit`.
- Chemistry starts at a neutral 50 and adds pair bonuses, capped at +32. Exact org-year teammate pairs add 4, any other pair that appeared together on a committed org-year roster adds 2.5, and same-nationality pairs add 2 (stacking with teammate bonuses). Centered contribution to overall OVR is weighted at 0.08, so an intact five helps without eclipsing individual quality.
- Communication blends a conservative nationality-to-language-family heuristic with the IGL-slot player's `igl` attribute (72% language / 28% IGL). Same-nationality pairs score 1.0, a documented family/English bridge scores 0.92, and unknown pairs retain a 0.68 international baseline. The coach contributes 10% of the language portion.
- Structure rewards primary and secondary role coverage, applies a strong penalty when the IGL-slot player has neither primary nor secondary IGL experience, and subtracts 6 points per redundant primary AWPer.
- Overall OVR starts at base strength and adds centered chemistry (`0.08`), communication (`0.05`), and structure (`0.12`) contributions. Coach modifiers add `0.25` OVR each while also shaping comeback resilience, economy discipline, and anti-strat scores.
- Coach cards store integer modifiers (−2 to +2) on comeback, economy, and antistrat. Import derives them from that coach's best roster placement: champion 4 points, finalist 3, top four 2, everyone else 1, distributed across the three axes (max 2 each) from a stable hash of the coach id so a last-place card still grants one bonus. Hand-tuned rows that do not match that formula are preserved, including `tier: "legendary"` cards. Coaching score is `50 + modifier-sum × 5`.

## Match simulation

`simulateSeries` is the batch helper under `src/engine/sim`. Live play uses `startLiveSeries` / `playRound` / `startNextMap` / `skipRemaining`. A given seed plus the same map queue and per-map game plans produce the same series, including when the coach calls the timeout. Live tournament maps are a seed shuffle from the pool (BO1 one map, BO3 three, no repeats, no home bonus). `SeriesFormat` also includes `BO5` (five maps, first to three). A supplied `playerMapId` still becomes map 1 with a home bonus for engine tests and the exhibition helper. Optional `MapContext.pickedBy` (`0` \| `1`) applies the same `±0.03` home edge to either side; omitted `pickedBy` keeps the old `homePick` boolean (team 0 only). Aim maps weight rifling; tactical maps weight IGL and coach anti-strat.

- Game plans (`standard`, `rush`, `late-execute`, `pick-heavy`, `executes`, `anti-strat`, `contact`, `risky`) still exist in the engine and `GamePlanPicker`, but the live UI does not show them. Tournament play uses Standard. Other plans shift fit-adjusted attributes plus anti-strat, economy discipline, pistol/early-round bias, and (for contact) starting morale. Risky plays push smokes and 50/50s (higher entry, lower consistency). The opponent is not planned. A BO3 starts the next shuffled map on Standard after the map-win overlay. Skip finishes only the current map, then that overlay; leftover maps start on Standard.

- Regulation is MR12: sides switch after 12 rounds, first to 13 wins, and 12-12 enters overtime. Overtime runs MR3 six-round blocks, swaps after three, and ends when a team wins four rounds in a block. Twelve tied blocks trigger one deterministic full-buy safety round.
- Team A round probability is `0.5 + overall delta × 0.006 + tactical side matchup × 0.001 + CT edge ±0.018 + equipment delta × 0.48`, plus pistol, trailing-team comeback, home-pick (`+0.03`), morale (`delta × 0.0012`), a one-round timeout spike (`+0.04`), and any revealed player-trait `bonusWinProb`, clamped to `0.12–0.88`. Trait `bonusWinProb` is catalog `win-prob` plus `(combat scale − 1) × 0.045`, so a doubled player is about a timeout-sized round (`+4.5%`) and VAC Ban is about `−3.4%` every remaining round. Equipment strength is full buy `1.0`, force `0.7`, pistol `0.48`, eco `0.28`, so an eco against rifles is about a 15–20% chance when the teams are even.
- Economy starts at `$800` each regulation half. A full buy costs `$3600` against a `$3250` win plus a `$1250` kill-reward abstraction. Two consecutive wins fund the next full buy; after four or more wins a team can take two more full buys through losses before it has to eco or force. A loss pays the `$1400–$3400` bonus plus `$300`, and a first gun-round loss after several buys is a force or rebuy, not an eco. Coach economy discipline shifts buy thresholds. Overtime halves reset to `$10,000`. Playback shows two bars: this-round five-player equipment (pistol `$4000`, eco `$2500`, force `$11,000`, full buy `$22,500`) and leftover bank cash (the sim bank × 5). During a live round the bank is post-buy leftover; after the round settles it is `bankAfter`.
- Round winner is decided first. Kills are then an elimination sequence: losers always wipe, winners keep 1–5 alive, only living players can kill or assist, and kill order is the clock. Each player is assigned a weapon from that round's buy, side, and slot: full buys are AK/M4 (AUG/SG 553 uncommon), the AWP slot takes an AWP on most rifle rounds, and pistol/eco/force rounds use the matching pistol, SMG, and cheap-rifle pools. Negev and auto-snipers are vanishingly rare. Every round also stores a structured `summary` (eco steal, force convert, ace, clutch, 3k/4k, timeout payoff, pistol, overtime, clean sweep, or a site execute/hold). Site names are sampled from that map's named areas and are flavor, not a bombsite sim. Scoreboard ADR, KAST, and rating are rounded display estimates rather than extra simulation inputs. Rating is HLTV-style and centered on **1.00** for an average map (about 0.67 KPR/DPR, 75 ADR, 70% KAST); a typical board mixes sub-1.00 and 1.2+ rows instead of stacking everyone above 1.
- Morale starts from chemistry and coach comeback resilience. It does not decay toward 50. A round win recovers morale and a win streak stacks larger gains; a loss drops it and consecutive losses stack larger drops. A converted or denied clutch moves morale more than a standard round, scaled by 1vX size. Timeouts are automatic, one per map, never player-called. After a settled round the engine may queue the remaining timeout for the next round: a stronger coach (`coaching.score` blended with comeback resilience) waits for a trailing rifle-round streak, a close or late map, or overtime, and skips pistols, early single losses, and comfortable leads; a weaker coach panics sooner. Calling it queues `+10` morale on the player's side for the next round. The huddle overlay and visible bar bump wait until the in-progress round's playback has settled. The one-round win-probability spike (`+0.04`) still applies only to the next simulated round. `queueTimeout` remains a test helper and is not wired to the UI.
- Highlights are seeded facts tied to generated rounds. A map stages at most three featured `clutch-sequence` 1vX windows, one in each early/mid/late stretch (rounds 1–8, 9–16, and 17+), and only for the player's side. The featured slot is offered uniformly across the remaining rounds in that stretch so the first round of a window is not favored. Under that cap the sim offers a 1v5 / 1v4 / 1v3 / 1v2 at about 5% / 20% / 40% / 60% (rarest first; first hit wins), then generates that window so a player-round win is a converted clutch. A player-round loss only becomes a featured denial at 1v3 or larger, and the clutcher still gets at least one kill before being shut down. Ordinary leftover 1vX endings stay at 1v3 or smaller and are not staged; 1v4 and 1v5 only appear when a featured window stages them. Opponent clutches are not staged. Revealed player traits (bonuses and maluses) each roll 2% independently on the player's team in starter-slot order; at most one new proc per round, opponents never proc. Duel series pass `bothSidesPlayer: true` so both coaches can timeout and both rosters can proc (still one new proc per round); featured 1vX staging stays team-0-only. Solo Major omits that flag so Daily and Free Play results do not move. Instant effects apply that round; Mastermind and The Professor linger at `+3.5%` win chance, Tactical Genius at `+3%` plus `+15` morale on proc, and VAC Ban keeps combat `0.25` for the rest of the current map. Combat-only cards also move the round through the combat→win-prob rule; morale cards add a same-round win spike (`+3%` / `+4%`, or `−2.5%` / `−4%` for maluses). Olofboost is `+12%` on eco or force. Timeouts stay orthogonal. `RoundResult.bonus` is set only for a new overlay-worthy proc. BO3 stops immediately at two map wins; BO5 at three. All randomness shares the supplied Mulberry32 stream; `rng.state` is serialized so a mid-match reload does not reroll played rounds.

## Major tournament

`createTournament`, `beginNextMatch`, `commitLiveMatch`, and `runNextMatch` under `src/engine/tournament` form the pure tournament state machine. `summarizeTournamentRun` folds history into a recap (finish label, maps/rounds, per-player scoreboard, MVP) without touching storage. State is serialization-safe and carries the root seed, both Swiss records, playoff round, next-match metadata, optional in-progress `liveSeries`, and complete `SeriesResult` history. Invalid transitions return `TournamentError` results; all match and opponent seeds derive from the root seed.

- The deliberate fantasy format is Challengers Swiss, Legends Swiss, then Champions quarterfinal/semifinal/final. Three Swiss wins advance, three losses eliminate, and the perfect title path is exactly 9–0.
- Swiss matches are BO1 unless either team-facing record is on two wins or two losses; advancement and elimination matches are BO3. Every playoff match is BO3 and a playoff loss eliminates.
- `buildHistoricalOpponents` turns every Major/Legacy roster into a natural best-fit role assignment, labels it with its event, snapshots the org (including logo path when imported), tags `source: "historical"`, and returns a stable strength-sorted index. It uses that roster's coach when available and deterministically selects a compatible fallback otherwise.
- Opponent choice is deterministic and uses each roster's `profile.overall` (the same team-strength score as the player's squad). Selection walks a rising percentile of that strength-sorted index: Challengers `0.14/0.28/0.42` by Swiss wins, Legends `0.55/0.65/0.75`, then Champions quarterfinal `0.84`. The semifinal picks from the 88–90 band (`SEMI_OPPONENT_OVERALL_FLOOR` / `SEMI_OPPONENT_OVERALL_CEILING`). The grand final floors the unused pool at 90 overall (`FINAL_OPPONENT_OVERALL_FLOOR`) and may use a stacked roster up to 100; 90+ teams are reserved for that title match. The next opponent is never weaker than the previous one when the unused pool allows; jitter only samples a small band above the target. Identical player rosters are excluded when alternatives exist, and immediate repeats are avoided whenever the pool permits.
- Versus community uses the same machine with a mixed pool: published community teams (`source: "community"`) plus historical fill. Strength still walks the historical curve through Swiss and the quarterfinal, so a stacked published roster does not jump the ladder early. After unused/no-repeat/floor filters, a community row is preferred only when its overall is within 3 of that historical target (`COMMUNITY_OVERALL_SLACK`), not weaker than the previous opponent, and not above the semifinal ceiling; otherwise the historical pick is used. The semifinal prefers a community roster in the 88–90 band. The final prefers a remaining community 90+ roster and is the only match that can face a 95–100 stack.
- Free Play persists completed drafts, tournaments (including an in-progress live series), and the chosen team name under `major-winners:tournament:v2`. Versus community uses `major-winners:community:v1` with the same document shape. Custom Game is a Free Play start with a player-supplied seed: uint32 digits stay numeric, any other string is FNV-1a hashed, and the run then persists as Free Play.

## Duel

Private match (home label; engine and storage still say Duel) is a casual 1v1, not Versus community. Ranked match is a signed-in 1v1 on the same rooms and veto path; it does not use a lobby code.

Casual: host creates a 6-character lobby; guest joins with the code. `join_duel` rejects `kind = ranked`. Ranked: `queue_ranked_match` (authenticated) heartbeats `ranked_queue`, matches the closest Elo in a window that starts at ±100 and grows by 50 every 5s of wait (cap ±400), skipping heartbeats older than 15s, then writes a ranked `duels` row and `ranked_assignments` secrets. `leave_ranked_queue` drops the waiter. First ranked queue creates a `profiles` row (handle 3–16 `[A-Za-z0-9_]`, unique case-insensitive, Elo 1000). Clients poll the queue every `DUEL_POLL_MS` like they poll the room.

Each player drafts independently (separate seeds), names the team, and locks a roster snapshot. That draft is bound to the lobby: New draft / Start another draft are not offered, so Continue private match / Continue ranked match cannot reroll the opener. Two-click Abandon run (draft, lock-in, or veto) or Leave match (playback) clears the local save and returns home; a fresh draft requires a new lobby (or a new ranked queue). After both lock, they veto the 9-map pool async: host ban, guest ban, host pick, guest pick, host ban, guest ban, host pick, guest pick, leftover decider. Play order is pick order then decider. Each pick grants that picker the `±0.03` home edge; the decider has none. The room stores a canonical `map_queue` and `series_seed`. Both clients run the same `startLiveSeries({ format: "BO5", bothSidesPlayer: true, mapQueue })` locally (host is always `teams[0]`). Playback is not lockstep. After the last map overlay, a recap names the winner and loser, the series and map scores, and both rosters. Ranked recaps also show handles and the Elo delta once both reports agree. Save team publishes that roster (not a Major run). **Back to Home** clears the local save. Casual signed-in W-L inserts into `duel_results`. Ranked calls `submit_ranked_result`; Elo (K=32, floor 100, binary series W/L) and current win streak are SQL-only and applied once when both claims are complementary, which also inserts both `duel_results` rows. Secrets live in `major-winners:duel:v1` with kind, draft, and optional live series. Mutations are `create_duel`, `join_duel`, `fetch_duel`, `submit_duel_roster`, `submit_duel_veto`, plus ranked `queue_ranked_match`, `leave_ranked_queue`, `submit_ranked_result`. No auto-pick if someone AFKs mid-veto. Pure veto rules live in `src/engine/sim/veto.ts`; the RPC must use the same ban/pick order. The TS helper in `src/community/elo.ts` mirrors the formula for tests and display; SQL remains authoritative.

## Daily challenge

The home screen is a centered brand mark (`public/logo.png`, also the favicon) with Today's Challenge, Free Play, Custom game seed, Community, and a half-width My profile / Leaderboards row (Leaderboards only when Supabase is configured; without it My profile is full width), on a frosted darkened panel over a fullscreen muted CS highlight reel. Two stacked `<video>` elements (`HomeHighlightReel`) cut at clip boundaries so `HOME_HIGHLIGHTS` (`public/home/video1.mp4`–`video6.mp4`) loops like one tape. Each visit starts on a random clip, then walks the rest in order. The footage stays sharp at the edges; it goes soft only under the menu, Custom game seed, Community, Private match / join lobby, Ranked handle / queue, Sign in, My profile, and Leaderboards overlays. `prefers-reduced-motion` skips the reel. The layout viewport uses `viewport-fit=cover`; fixed home logo, Sign in, and recap **Back to Home** offset with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`. A small Sign in control sits at the top right of the home viewport when community is configured and there is no session. My profile is a menu button (beside Leaderboards when that board is shown) so local stats stay reachable without a session. My profile holds browser-only stats (total Daily match wins, Daily runs completed, perfect 9–0s, most-used player by career id) and, when signed in, ranked Elo/handle/W-L plus win rate and current win streak, and the user's top three published teams ranked like Best runs. Leaderboards is a wide home overlay with **Best runs**, **Highest rated teams**, and **Highest Elo** (top 10 each): compact year portraits with nicks for team boards, the UTC save date, and (for runs) wins then fewer losses/maps/rounds, (for teams) recomputed OVR, (for Elo) handle and ranked W-L. Profile and leaderboards overlays scroll inside the frosted card on short viewports. Sign-in is optional. During a run the same mark sits at the top left of the viewport and returns to that home screen without wiping a saved Daily, Free Play, Versus, or Private/ranked match attempt. A finished recap shows **Back to Home** at the top right of the viewport, the same rounded emerald control as Continue Major; that control clears the saved attempt and returns to home instead of starting a replacement draft. Abandoning an in-progress Major run (two-click confirm on the tournament board) or a Private/ranked match (two-click Abandon run / Leave match) also clears that saved attempt and returns to home. Continue private match / Continue ranked match resumes the same lobby draft and does not offer a new one. Today's Challenge, Free Play, and Versus start the draft immediately. Custom game seed asks for a seed. Community opens Ranked match, Private match, or Free play with community teams. Private match then chooses create a lobby or join a lobby; a saved Private or ranked match resumes immediately. Ranked match requires sign-in and a handle, then polls for a nearby Elo opponent. The team name is requested on the locked-in screen after five players and a coach are picked, before Start Major. Saved attempts resume immediately and keep their team name when one was already chosen. A finished Major run or private/ranked match can **Save team**: roster + coach + traits (and, for a Major, that run's record), with an optional author name (default Anonymous).

Today's Challenge uses the UTC day and `seedFromUtcDate`, making the draft, coach order, and opponents deterministic for identical choices. Match results are deterministic for the same map queue and per-map game plans (coach timeouts included). Restarting uses the same daily seed; Free Play remains random unless started as a Custom Game.

- Pure identity, stats, result, and spoiler-free share calculations live in `src/engine/daily/`. Completed Daily results may store the five season ids so most-used player can be counted; older rows without a roster omit that field.
- Daily attempts use `major-winners:daily-attempt:v2` (including the chosen team name); local stats use `major-winners:daily-stats:v1`. Rerolled cards and remaining budgets survive reloads. Both are separate from Free Play, Versus, and Duel (`major-winners:duel:v1`).
- Published-run fingerprints are remembered in `major-winners:published-fingerprints:v1` so a reload does not double-submit the same payload.
- Signed-in private match results persist in `duel_results` and are unique per user and room code. Ranked Elo, W-L, and streak are not written from that client insert; `submit_ranked_result` applies both.
- One terminal result is counted per UTC day. Streaks use consecutive UTC completion days across calendar boundaries.
- Storage, clipboard, and native-share side effects stay in components. Defensive parsing and unavailable storage never block play.
- Static social metadata uses the generic `/social-card.svg`; no dynamic daily Open Graph image is claimed.

## Data conventions

- The draftable atom is a player-org-season (`s1mple-2018-navi`), not a career. This permits same-year transfers.
- `majors.json` contains 24 completed Valve Majors through Cologne 2026. `org-years.json` contains 511 teams that actually played plus two Legacy wildcards; replaced source cards are not game entities.
- Every roster appearance has exactly five starters, optional registered substitutes, placement, Major/Legacy identity, and source metadata. Placement is the start of the Liquipedia prize-pool range (3 for 3rd–4th, 5 for 5th–8th). Roles are inferred or curated; TeamCard order is not a role. Off-role placement is always available.
- `npm run import:majors -- --write` performs the one-time, rate-limited Liquipedia MediaWiki import and caches source revisions under ignored `.cache/`. The shipped app has no HLTV/Liquipedia runtime network dependency. Community boards use Supabase when configured.
- `npm run import:hltv` resolves player ids from HLTV search JSON, then scrapes year-filtered `/stats/players` pages in headed Chrome. Calls are serialized at a 20s default gap into ignored `.cache/hltv-import/`. Cache hits are skipped, so a stopped run can be rerun. `--apply` promotes complete fallback rows into committed `player-seasons.json`; verified rows are never overwritten, and curated roles and imported photos are never changed.
- Org logos are committed files under `public/logos/{orgId}.png`, referenced by the `org.logo` path. Major logos are committed under `public/logos/majors/{majorId}.{ext}`, referenced by `major.logo`. The pre-2013 Legacy wildcard is not a Major row; its crest is `public/logos/majors/cs16.png`. `npm run import:logos -- --write` reparses the cached rendered Major pages, prefers each team's dark-mode team-template icon and each event's dark-mode infobox image, downloads them rate-limited, and rewrites `orgs.json` and `majors.json`. Logos are third-party marks, not part of the CC BY-SA roster data. An org or Major without a logo falls back to a generated initials crest.
- Optional map art lives under `public/maps/{mapId}.webp`, resized to 1920px-wide WebP. The pool is Mirage, Dust II, Inferno, Nuke, Ancient, Anubis, Overpass, Cache, and Cobblestone. The picker and match board both use each map's committed `background` path. Missing files fall back to the tinted match board. `npm run optimize:maps` recompresses replacements. Tournament play preloads the pool so the first match board does not wait on a 4K PNG.
- Home highlight clips live under `public/home/` as H.264 MP4s. The ordered playlist is `HOME_HIGHLIGHTS` in `src/components/homeHighlights.ts`. Each visit starts on a random clip. The reel is muted and presentation-only; `prefers-reduced-motion` skips it.
- Killfeed weapon silhouettes live under `public/weapons/`. Filenames follow the weapon id except M4A1-S (`m4a1.png`), SSG 08 (`ssg.png`), SG 553 (`sg_553.png`), sawed-off (`saw.png`), auto-sniper (`auto.png`), and USP-S (`usps.png`). Glock has no file and uses a generic pistol mark. CT pistol rounds already prefer the USP-S.
- Player-year portraits are committed under `public/photos/players/{playerId}/{year}.{ext}`, referenced by optional `playerSeason.photo`. `npm run import:photos` reuses cached HLTV identities and headed Chrome, then opens the same year-filtered `/stats/players` URL as the stats importer and reads `img.player-summary-stat-box-left-bodyshot` (`img-cdn.hltv.org/playerbodyshot/…`). That asset is year-specific. Resolved URLs are cached under `.cache/hltv-import/photos/`. `--write` downloads the file and rewrites `player-seasons.json`. Two org appearances in the same year share one file. A season without a portrait keeps the initials crest. Majors import and `hltv --apply` must not strip photos.
- JSON lives in `src/data/json/` under the separate `DATA-LICENSE.md` terms. Contracts live in `src/data/schema.ts`. Player-season bonuses and maluses are a sidecar `player-bonuses.json` (season id → one or more catalog ids); badges share `public/bonuses/bonus.svg` and `public/bonuses/malus.svg`, and overlay art lives under `public/bonuses/art/{id}.webp`. Provenance and the transcription workflow live in `docs/DATA.md`.
- `npm run validate-data` must stay green. It enforces regime consistency, 24 revision-pinned Majors, each played field size, five unique starters, relational integrity, and that every referenced logo, player photo, bonus icon, map-art file, and home highlight clip exists.
